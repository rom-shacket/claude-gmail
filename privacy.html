Privacy policy

Claude Gmail is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google accounts.

Google account data accessed through this app is used only to let the owner read, search, organize and send their own email from their own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. The use of data obtained from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access at any time, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "Claude Gmail".
